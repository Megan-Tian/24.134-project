Imagine that you are a parent who is solely responsible for your family's livelihood.

One day, you learn about a vacant position in another division that provides a better salary than your current position. Consider your opinion about the level of your income.

A few days later, you learn about a vacant position at another branch office that requires a shorter commute than your current position. Consider your opinion about the distance you travel to work.

Your boss then tells you that the division where you work will close, but intends to continue employing you. You are offered the same two jobs you previously considered.

The first offer provides a better salary but requires a longer commute because it is located at another branch.

The second offer requires a shorter commute but provides a lower salary.

You must decide between:

Option 1: Choose the job with the better salary.
Option 2: Choose the job with the shorter commute.
