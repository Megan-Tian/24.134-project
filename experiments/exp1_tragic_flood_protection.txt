Imagine that you are the president of the local authority of a village that has been severely affected by a flood.

The local authority is considering whether to invest a substantial portion of its annual budget in improved flood-protection measures. Doing so would mean giving up a planned project providing vocational training and integration for unemployed adolescents.

As president, you must decide between:

Option 1: Improve flood protection.
Option 2: Fund the vocational-training and integration project.
