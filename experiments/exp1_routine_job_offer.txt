Imagine that you are a parent who is solely responsible for your family's livelihood. You have applied for several jobs and have now received two offers.

Company A offers an annual salary of CHF 80,000 [USD 66,000] and 20 vacation days per year.

Company B offers an annual salary of CHF 60,000 [USD 50,000] and 30 vacation days per year.

You must decide between:

Option 1: Choose the job with the higher annual salary.
Option 2: Choose the job with the greater number of vacation days.
