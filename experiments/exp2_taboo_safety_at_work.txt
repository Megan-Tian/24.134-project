Imagine that you are the CEO of a global company that has been criticized for poor working conditions in a Chinese factory.

You are attending a management meeting. The members are discussing whether measures should be taken to improve safety at work. Consider your position on improving safety at work because there will be a vote at the end of the meeting.

Before the final vote, other topics are discussed.

Your company is under pricing pressure because many competitors have a higher production volume. To reduce prices, it has been proposed that production should be increased through additional facilities. Consider your position on increasing production through additional facilities because there will be a vote at the end of the meeting.

At the end of the meeting, both proposals have been approved: improving workplace safety and increasing production. Implementing both projects would exceed the available budget.

As CEO, you must make the final choice between:

Option 1: Invest in safety at work.
Option 2: Increase production through additional facilities.
