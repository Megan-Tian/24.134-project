Imagine that you are a member of the local authority of a village that has been severely affected by a flood.

You are attending a meeting of the authority. The members are discussing whether measures to improve flood protection should be taken. Consider your position on improving flood protection because there will be a vote at the end of the meeting.

Before the final vote, other topics are discussed.

The village has experienced increasing juvenile violence and delinquency. The authority is discussing whether a project providing vocational training and integration for unemployed adolescents should be launched. Consider your position on this project because there will be a vote at the end of the meeting.

At the end of the meeting, both proposals have been approved: improving flood protection and launching the vocational-training and integration project. Implementing both projects would exceed the available budget.

As president, you must make the final choice between:

Option 1: Improve flood protection.
Option 2: Fund the vocational-training and integration project.
