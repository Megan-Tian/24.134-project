Imagine that you are the president of the local authority of a village that has been severely affected by a flood.

You are attending a meeting of the authority. The members are discussing whether measures to improve flood protection should be taken. Consider your position on improving flood protection because there will be a vote at the end of the meeting.

Before the final vote, other topics are discussed.

Your village square is in poor condition because of unauthorized parking and damaged paving. The authority is discussing whether measures should be taken to renovate the village square. Consider your position on renovating the village square because there will be a vote at the end of the meeting.

At the end of the meeting, both proposals have been approved: improving flood protection and renovating the village square. Implementing both projects would exceed the available budget.

As president, you must make the final choice between:

Option 1: Improve flood protection.
Option 2: Renovate the village square.
